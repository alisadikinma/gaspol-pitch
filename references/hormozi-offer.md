# Alex Hormozi — Grand Slam Offer / Value Equation (reference)

> Framework distilled from Alex Hormozi's "$100M Offers". Use it to sharpen the
> **value & proof** on every pitch slide — NOT to turn the deck into a hard-sell
> ad. Investors judge fundability and vision; Hormozi here is a lens for making
> the value undeniable and the evidence concrete.

## Core: the Grand Slam Offer

"Make people an offer so good they feel stupid saying no." Offer = combination of
**pricing + value + guarantees + naming** that can't be compared to anything else
in the market (a *category of one*). For a pitch, the "offer" is your
value proposition to the customer AND your investment proposition to the investor.

## 1. Starving Crowd > Offer > Persuasion

Pick a market with 4 indicators: **(1) Massive Pain, (2) Purchasing Power,
(3) Easy to Target, (4) Growing.** The right market matters more than offer
strength. → maps directly to the Problem + Market slides.

## 2. The Value Equation (the heart of the pitch)

```
            Dream Outcome  ×  Perceived Likelihood of Achievement
Value =  ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
            Perceived Time Delay  ×  Perceived Effort & Sacrifice
```

- **Raise:** Dream Outcome (gap between dream and reality) + Likelihood (proof the result is near-certain).
- **Lower:** Time Delay (fast time-to-value) + Effort/Sacrifice (done-for-you > done-with-you > do-it-yourself).
- → Every slide should move at least one of these 4 variables. Traction raises Likelihood; a fast onboarding lowers Time Delay; an outcome headline raises Dream Outcome.

## 3. Build the offer: Problems → Solutions

- Step 1: Identify the **Dream Outcome** (what the customer experiences when they arrive).
- Step 2: List **every obstacle** in the way.
- Step 3: Turn each obstacle into a **named solution**.

## 4. Trim & Stack

- Delivery cheat-codes: Attention (1:1 / group / one-to-many), Effort (DIY/DWY/DFY), Support, Consumption, Speed.
- **Trim:** drop high-cost/low-value and low-value items. Keep low-cost-high-value + high-cost-high-value.
- **Stack:** combine the high-value items into a package nothing else compares to.
- Mantra: **Create flow → Monetize flow → Add friction.**

## 5. Enhancers

- **Scarcity** (limit quantity): cohort cap, growth-rate cap, honesty. "Fear of loss > desire for gain."
- **Urgency** (limit time): rolling cohorts, deadlines, an exploding opportunity (this maps to "Why Now").
- **Bonuses:** break the offer into named components, each priced; each bonus solves one specific obstacle.
- **Guarantees** (reverse the risk): "If you don't get X in Y, we Z." Performance/service-based are strongest. In a pitch, this maps to milestone commitments and de-risking the investment.
- **Naming (MAGIC):** Magnetic reason · Announce the avatar · Goal · Interval · Container word.

## 6. How to apply to a deck

1. Run a **10-bullet analysis** (5 strengths + 5 weaknesses) of the business through the value lens.
2. Sketch **3 versions** of the value proposition, each emphasizing a different part of the Value Equation, then pick the sharpest.
3. On each slide, ask: *which of the 4 value variables does this move, and is the proof concrete?*
